Usage guidelines

Free to use for editorial coverage, partner integrations, and reviews of the NordInvest property investment analyser. Please respect the mark.

Do

  • Use the light variant on light backgrounds, dark variant on dark backgrounds
  • Preserve at least 20 % clear space around the logo
  • Link the logo to https://www.nordinvest.io when used online
  • Credit as "NordInvest" (one word, capital N and I)
  • Scale proportionally — never distort or stretch

Don't

  • Recolor the logo or apply gradients, shadows, or effects
  • Rotate, skew, or crop the mark inside the icon
  • Combine the logo with other brands into a lockup without permission
  • Use the logo in a way that implies endorsement of unrelated products
  • Reproduce the logo below 32 px on screen or 12 mm in print

About NordInvest

NordInvest is a free property investment analyser built for the Nordic and European markets. Users paste a listing URL and receive rental yield, cash flow, ROI, BRRRR / renovation projection, a 5-year appreciation forecast, and a strategy-aware verdict in under 60 seconds. Local market data is sourced exclusively from official national statistics agencies — Danmarks Statistik (DST EJ55) for Denmark, Statistiska centralbyrån (SCB BO0501) for Sweden, and Statistisk sentralbyrå (SSB 06035) for Norway. Available in English and Danish at nordinvest.io.
