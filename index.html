FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about analyzing property investments with NordInvest.

How do I know if a property is a good investment?
Evaluate it across four wealth engines: rental cash flow, long-term price appreciation, value-add potential, and financing leverage. A good investment has at least one strong engine and survives an interest-rate stress test. NordInvest analyzes all four and classifies the property automatically in under 60 seconds.
What is a good rental yield in Denmark, Norway, or Sweden?
In major Nordic cities, gross rental yields typically range from about 3% to 4.5%. These are appreciation-driven markets, so a lower yield is normal and not automatically bad. Yields above roughly 5.5% usually indicate a cash-flow-focused property in a secondary market. Learn how to calculate rental yield →
Is negative monthly cash flow always a bad sign?
No. In low-yield, high-appreciation markets like Copenhagen, a slightly negative monthly cash flow can still be an excellent long-term investment if the area appreciates steadily and you can hold for several years. What matters is whether the cash flow is fundable and survives a higher-interest-rate scenario.
Is NordInvest free to use?
Yes. NordInvest is completely free and requires no sign-up. Paste a property listing URL or enter the numbers manually to get rental yield, cash flow, ROI, a 5-year appreciation projection, and an investment classification instantly.
