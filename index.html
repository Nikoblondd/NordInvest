Features

Everything you need to evaluate a property

Professional-grade investment analysis, available to everyone.

Investment Score

A single 0–100 score combining yield, cash flow, and ROI into one clear verdict on a property's investment potential.

Rental Yield

Calculates gross rental yield as annual income divided by property price — the core profitability metric used by professionals.

Cash Flow Analysis

Monthly income minus mortgage and expenses. Know exactly how much cash a property puts in your pocket each month.

ROI Calculator

Return on investment based on annual profit versus cash actually invested — the true measure of investment efficiency.

Local Market Research

Automatically researches comparable rental prices in your target area to benchmark your expected rent against the market.

AI Summary

A plain-English summary of the property's strengths, weaknesses, and risks — written by AI, based on the numbers.
